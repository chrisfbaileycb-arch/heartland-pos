# Channel Cutover Instructions
**Signal F Holdings LLC — For Client Use**

---

These instructions are for you to keep. When you are ready to switch from your current menu to the newly built version, follow these steps at your own pace. There is no deadline.

---

## Before You Switch

You should feel completely confident in your v2 channels before cutting over. That means:

- You have placed test orders on every v2 channel and verified the results
- Your staff has had a chance to see the new menu layout
- Any corrections you requested have been applied and re-verified
- You have a quiet moment to do this — not during a Friday dinner rush

**Recommended time:** Early morning before opening, or on a slow day.

---

## The Switch (Channel by Channel)

Switch one channel at a time. Do not switch all channels simultaneously.

**Recommended order:**
1. Carry-Out v2 first (lowest risk, easy to verify in real time)
2. Pick-Up v2
3. Online v2
4. Dine-In v2 last (most complex)

### For Each Channel:

**Step 1:** Activate the v2 channel in Heartland  
**Step 2:** Place one real test order on the new channel  
**Step 3:** Verify the order came through correctly  
**Step 4:** Resume normal operations on the new channel  
**Step 5:** Leave the old channel available — do not delete it yet  

---

## Your Safety Net

Your original channels (Dine-In, Carry-Out, etc.) remain fully intact and available for a minimum of two weeks after cutover. If anything feels wrong on v2, you can switch back to v1 instantly — no call to us required.

**To switch back:** Simply stop routing orders to the v2 channel and resume on the original. That's it.

---

## Retiring the Old Channels

Only retire (delete) your v1 channels when you are completely satisfied with v2 and have been running on it successfully. We recommend waiting at least two weeks.

When you are ready to retire v1:
- Export a final copy of the v1 menu configuration for your records
- Delete the v1 channels from Heartland
- Contact Signal F Holdings LLC to confirm the transition is complete

---

## If Something Doesn't Look Right

Contact Christopher Bailey — Signal F Holdings LLC  
Available during business hours for transition support.

Do not attempt to edit the v2 menu yourself during the transition period. If a correction is needed, contact us and we will apply it to v2 before you go live on that channel.

## What Changed (Summary)

Your new menu has been rebuilt with:

- **Isolated modifier groups** — each ingredient belongs to the items that actually use it. Disabling one ingredient no longer affects unrelated dishes.
- **Corrected time ranges** — any pricing rules that crossed midnight have been properly split so they activate correctly.
- **Consolidated modifier entries** — modifiers that were entered manually many times have been replaced with properly configured groups. Price changes now only need to be made in one place.
- **Corporate-grade menu architecture** — the same modifier group structure used by chain restaurants, applied to your independent operation.
